# What I do
Finetune a cover letter and CV based on the existing cover letters and CVs already created and revised by the user.
Take a look at the other cover letters and CVs in the subdirectories in `Jobs`. 
Use the same writing style, the same structure and similar wording to adjust the target cover letter.
Place more emphasis on cover letters from similar positions. You might check the job descriptions of the other positions.

# When to use me
Use this skill after you initially drafted a cover letter to revise the initial draft.
